resumo: "We woke up and had breakfast around eight, and an hour and a quarter later set off on an easy, relaxed walk with a guide — one of the activities the lodge offers, lasting around an hour and a half. Back by mid-morning, the rest of the day was entirely our own. After the pace of the previous days, that was exactly what was needed.

The lodge has two pools: a regular one, on the cooler side, and a natural pool fed by a hot spring running at around 36°C. We used both, which in Namibian winter felt like a genuine luxury. Lunch was provided by the lodge around one. The afternoon stayed free — board games, cards, reading, rest. Dinner at half past seven, followed by more time to talk before turning in.

The most relaxed day of the trip. Not every day needs to be a highlight."
